🛡️ The 5 Verification Gates (ALL must pass for repair release)

  1. Gate 1 (Applied Guard): Repair transformation was actually computed and not bypassed.
  2. Gate 2 (No Label Flip): Predicted diagnosis does not flip across the 0.5 decision threshold (no hallucination).
  3. Gate 3 (OOD Invariance): Repaired image remains strictly within IN_DISTRIBUTION space.
  4. Gate 4 (Quality Resolution): Image quality must transition to GOOD (improving from POOR/DEGRADED).
  5. Gate 5 (Non-Degradation): Confidence loss cannot exceed 1% (ΔConf ≥ −0.01) and SNR loss cannot exceed 5 dB.

💬 Defense Q&A (Frequently Asked by Examiners)

Oral Exam Ready
Q1: Why use a multi-agent system instead of training an end-to-end model?
A single neural network is a black box. If it fails, clinicians cannot know whether it failed due to blur, unfamiliar scanner equipment, or clinical ambiguity. Our multi-agent architecture isolates concerns: each agent has a transparent, testable mathematical responsibility.
Q2: Why is patient-strict dataset splitting mandatory?
A patient often has multiple X-rays taken over time. If images from the same patient exist in both train and test splits, the model memorizes anatomical patient quirks rather than disease features, causing massive optimistic data leakage. We enforce 0 patient overlap across all 112,120 images.
Q3: How does the Mahalanobis distance detect Out-of-Distribution images?
Euclidean distance assumes spherical variance. Mahalanobis distance measures how many standard deviations away an image's 1024-D feature vector is from the 78k training mean, accounting for feature correlations using the regularized inverse covariance matrix solved via Cholesky decomposition.
Q4: Why is the Negative Predictive Value (99.02%) the most crucial metric here?
In a triage setting with 1.28% pneumonia prevalence, missing a sick patient (False Negative) can be fatal. A 99.02% NPV means that when our system releases a "No Pneumonia" prediction, it is correct 99% of the time, safely offloading radiologist workload while escalating positive and borderline cases for expert review.
